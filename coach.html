Background

From The Platform
To The Floor

Arjun spent six years competing in raw powerlifting before moving fully into coaching. He built his first programming system out of necessity — for himself, on a kitchen table, between two jobs.

That system is now the backbone of every plan he writes: patient, unglamorous, and built around the lifts you'll still be doing in ten years.

Three Habits
Behind Every Plan

The same three questions run behind every program Arjun writes.

Habit

Measure First

No program starts before a real baseline — strength, mobility, recovery, schedule.

Habit

Write By Hand

Every block is written for the athlete in front of him, never copied from a template.

Habit

Adjust Weekly

Plans move with how you're actually recovering, not just how the spreadsheet says you should.

Habit

Coach The Person

Sleep, stress, and schedule shape the program as much as the lifts themselves.
