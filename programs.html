How It Starts

The First
Four Weeks

Every athlete moves through the same onboarding sequence, regardless of tier.

01

Baseline Assessment

Strength testing, mobility screen, training history, and a full conversation about schedule, recovery, and goals.

02

Program Written

Arjun builds your first four-week block by hand, calibrated to the baseline — never a copied template.

03

Week One Calibration

Loads and volume get adjusted after your first sessions — the plan meets you where you actually are.

04

First Review

At week four, you and Arjun review progress together and plan the next training block.
